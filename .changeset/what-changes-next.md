`now` says what changes next: one line under today's range when, in the next 12 hours, rain or snow becomes likely or eases, or strong gusts start, or when tomorrow is at least 5° warmer or colder. The thresholds are fixed and the forecast's probability is always shown. `now --json` has the same as a `changes` array, and hourly JSON gains `windGusts`.
