Redrawn weather pictures: a crescent moon, a sun rising behind the partly cloudy sky, two cloud banks for overcast, rain centred under its cloud, and a lightning bolt that no longer looks like rain without colour. Showers and snow showers now show the sun or the stars of their clear spells, and hail has its own picture.

The hourly temperature chart no longer stretches a change of a degree or two to its full height, and an hourly window without temperatures no longer prints "Infinity". Settings written by a newer skycast are kept when an older one saves the file. JSON output now says why stale data is shown (`meta.staleBecause`).
